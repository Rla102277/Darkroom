# IA Kin — v1.2

Kin replaces IA 400H at ID 1007: luminous everyday color, soft greens, clear blues, lively warm colors and anchored blacks. The default is the 400H-led direction. Kin — Agfa is an alternative CUBE for comparison, not an additional installed slot.

Source: M9 Fuji 400H L dual-illuminant HueSat tables, bounded relative to a median of the available comparison profiles recorded in sources.json. The Agfa alternative blends 35% of the relative Ultra 100 table and adds selective chroma. Both use the same tone curve. Selected matching Leica 400H preset HSL adjustments are attenuated: hue 32%, saturation 40%, luminance 18%. Its global black lift, camera calibration controls, grain, split tone and channel curves are deliberately omitted. This is not an exact Lightroom preset conversion or calibrated film simulation. Relative table subtraction cannot uniquely separate camera calibration from creative behavior; linear-HSV data are adapted as artistic display-RGB changes.

No stacked pastel shaping or 7.5% white veil. A gentle shared curve anchors black near .004 and white near .994, with modest warmth in brighter tones. Previews numerically apply the final 17³ LUT to the supplied rendered PNG; camera rendering can differ. This scene cannot establish skin rendering; film reference scans are already graded and are not neutral calibration inputs.

Other eight Looks, icons and upload protocol are preserved. Both installers validate nine assets and generate identical payloads offline. No camera upload was performed.
