---
name: Editable graph authority
description: The durable source-of-truth rule for Look editing, previews, and exports.
---

An ordered, non-destructive component graph is the master definition of every custom Look. Source components retain their own provenance and editable strength, order, enabled, and solo state.

**Why:** Creators must be able to combine and revise parts from different profiles without flattening the work into an opaque LUT.

**How to apply:** Compile a CUBE for software preview or final export without replacing the graph. Leica payloads and downloadable packages are derived, validated artifacts; never treat them as the editable source of truth.
