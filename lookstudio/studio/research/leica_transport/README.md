# Historical Leica transport research

This directory preserves superseded camera-network work for forensic reference.
It is not imported, served, configured, or required by Infinite Arch Photo Lab.

The production product compiles and downloads files. It does not discover,
connect to, read from, write to, or verify a camera.

Authoritative v1.2 transport research also remains byte-for-byte inside the
immutable archive under `reference/leica/v1.2/`.
